    Never played? Start here

    Padel is easy to pick up. Most people are having proper rallies by the end of their first hour. Read the full rules.

    It's doubles

    Two against two, on a court about a third the size of a tennis court with glass and mesh walls all round. That's why you need four people.

    Walls are in play

    The ball can bounce once on the floor, then come off the glass, and you can still hit it. Learning to let it come off the back wall is the biggest "click" moment.

    Serve underarm

    Bounce the ball and hit it below waist height, diagonally into the box opposite. The scoring is the same as tennis: 15, 30, 40, game.

    What to bring

    Trainers with a flat, grippy sole (court or tennis shoes are best, not running shoes) and comfy sportswear. Most venues hire out rackets, and you'll need a tube of balls.

    Your first session

    Book a free or cheap intro session (see the deals board below). A coach will show you the basics, and you'll meet other beginners to play with.

    Finding a game

    Most clubs run socials and "Americanos", where you rotate partners. They're the easiest way to play without bringing three mates.
